Aetherfox collects nothing. There is no account to create and nothing to opt
out of, because there is nothing being sent.

## What is sent

Nothing. Aetherfox does not send the sites you visit, your searches, your
bookmarks, your passwords, your device identifier, crash reports, or usage
statistics. There is no telemetry server to receive them.

## What stays on your device

Bookmarks, passwords, history, cookies and site permissions are stored on your
machine and are never uploaded. Because there is no sync service, there is also
no copy of that data anywhere else.

## What the browser sends to the sites you visit

The usual, unavoidable requests: the page itself, the resources it loads, and
the address of the site you asked for. Aetherfox does not add anything to
those requests.

To reduce what those sites can learn, Aetherfox changes the values that
fingerprinting relies on — canvas pixels, text measurements, graphics card
details, audio analysis and processor core count — so each site sees slightly
different information.

## This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no
resources from third parties, including fonts, which are served from this
domain. Your only preference stored in the browser is the theme you picked.
