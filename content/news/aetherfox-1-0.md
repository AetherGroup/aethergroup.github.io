Aetherfox 1.0 is the first release you can hand to someone else and tell them
to use as their daily browser.

## What you get

- A Firefox engine that behaves exactly like the Firefox you know.
- No telemetry, no crash pings, no "help us improve" prompts.
- No account, no sync, no cloud profile. Your bookmarks and passwords stay on
  the machine they were created on.
- Fingerprinting resistance that changes what each site reads, so the picture
  never adds up.

## Getting it

Download the build for your platform, and import your bookmarks and passwords
when the browser opens for the first time. The import happens locally; nothing
is uploaded.
