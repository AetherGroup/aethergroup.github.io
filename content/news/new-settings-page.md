The old settings page grew one checkbox at a time until it stopped being scannable.
The new one starts from what people actually change.

## What changed

- **Grouped by task.** Everything now lives under a short list of plain-language
  sections instead of a tree that mirrored the code.
- **Search.** Type a few letters and matching settings come up.
- **Plain language.** "Send usage data" became "Send usage data to Aetherfox",
  with the consequences written underneath.

## What did not change

Nothing is sent anywhere. There is no toggle for telemetry, because there is
nothing to turn off. The only setting that touches the network is the one that
lets you point Aetherfox at your own search provider.
