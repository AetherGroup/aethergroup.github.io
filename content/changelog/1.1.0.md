## Added

- A rebuilt Shields panel that explains what each protection does instead of
  just listing its name.
- A setting to disable the fingerprinting resistance per site, for the rare
  page that breaks when values differ.

## Changed

- Cold start is 18% faster. Most of the saving comes from not loading the
  telemetry and crash-reporting subsystems at all.
- Settings now say what a toggle does before you flip it.

## Fixed

- The download panel no longer overlapped the Shields panel on narrow windows.
